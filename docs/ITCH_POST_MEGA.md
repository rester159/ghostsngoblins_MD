# From Ghosts'n Goblins MD to Mega Ghosts'n Goblins, and Firebrand's Vendetta

Hi everyone!

The project has a new name: **Mega Ghosts'n Goblins**. Here's why, and what's in it, mostly the new campaign, **Firebrand's Vendetta**.

## Why a new name?

Ghosts'n Goblins MD started as a repair job. The Mega Drive port only really behaved in forgiving emulators, so I fixed it to run on real hardware and accurate emulators. Then I made it faster. Then I added an Arcade/Home menu, DIP switches, saved high scores, co-op with **Maximo**, and a full campaign as **Firebrand**, the Red Arremer.

At some point it stopped being a fixed port and became its own game built on top of one. The Home side now has two Firebrand campaigns, its own options, parallax backgrounds and new art. So it gets its own name and logo: a **MEGA** in Demon's Crest-style lettering, with a demon silhouette wrapped around the original GHOSTS'N GOBLINS title. (Arcade mode stays the original, and the Japanese Makaimura title is still in the options.)

## Firebrand's Vendetta

Firebrand's Quest lets you play the arcade game as the Red Arremer. **Vendetta** is what happens after it, and it's the mode I'm proudest of.

### The story

For as long as the Demon Realm has stood, Arthur's lance has cut down Red Arremers. Firebrand thought it was war. Then, in Satan's throne room, he finds the truth carved on a sealed tablet: Satan made a pact with the knight. Every time Arthur came to "save" the princess, Satan opened the gates and gave him a road paved with Arremers. The Red clan was the price Satan paid to keep his throne.

So Firebrand kills the king.

In the Demon Realm, killing the king is the one crime nobody forgives. Every zombie, ogre, Big Man and flying knight was bound to Satan, and now every one of them wants Firebrand dead. He has no allies left. He has one name left: **Arthur**.

### The whole game, backwards

- **It opens on the final fight.** You start in stage 6's throne room against Satan, one-on-one.
- **The stages run in reverse, 6 down to 1, and each map is played right to left.** You start beside each stage's boss arena and fight your way back to where Arthur's run began. Arthur's long drops become your climbs.
- **A mirrored stage map:** Satan's lair on the left, the graveyard on the right, numbered in the order you play them.
- **The camera looks ahead.** It holds Firebrand farther right and higher up than Arthur, so you can see where you're flying and what's below you.
- **Everyone wants him dead:** Vendetta starts at the top difficulty rank and never drops below it.

### Flying, flapping and climbing

Firebrand moves like a Red Arremer now:

- **Flight:** jump, then press jump again in the air to hover. He holds his height and steers until the flight meter runs out (press DOWN to drop).
- **Flaps:** in Vendetta, jump in the air is also a wing flap, a smaller jump up. That's how you climb places like stage 2's ice towers, where there's no wall to hold.
- **Wall cling:** push into a wall in the air to grab it, then hop up it.
- **Wing pickups:** weapon drops become a pair of red wings. Each pair raises your flight level (up to 4), and a death costs one level.
- He shoots fireballs that burst on walls Demon's Crest-style, and he has no armour: **one hit and he's dead.**

### The last boss: Arthur

At the graveyard's far end, Arthur is waiting among the tombstones, in full armour. He knows the pact is broken and that an Arremer has come for him.

He's a real duel, not a reskinned enemy. He walks, jumps, ducks your fireballs, and throws lances standing, crouching and from the top of his jumps. Burn off his armour and he fights in his boxers: faster, closer, throwing aimed daggers and dodging even more. It's the hardest fight in the game.

Beat him and you get the ending, and your name on the high-score table.

## Thank you, Robot-Random and HJRodrigo 🎨

When I asked for help with the art, two people answered and made the game look so much better.

The Maximo and Firebrand frames started as script output: Arthur's frames recoloured, and a Demon's Crest sprite shrunk down until it went muddy. **Robot-Random** and **HJRodrigo** redrew them by hand in the pixel editors, one frame at a time, inside the game's palette and frame sizes. Maximo's final sprite sheet and the new Firebrand both come from that work, and both of them are credited on the COMMUNITY CREDITS screen.

Thank you both. Your work is a big part of why this feels like its own game now. 🙏

## Download & play

- **Emulators:** load the .bin in any Mega Drive / Genesis emulator with battery saves (tested in Genesis Plus GX and PicoDrive).
- **Flashcarts / real hardware:** the game saves to 8 KiB of battery SRAM on a 4 MiB cartridge, so your cart needs SRAM support at that size (most EverDrives have it). It still hasn't been tested on a real console, so reports are very welcome.
- **To play Vendetta:** pick **HOME**, then **FIREBRAND'S VENDETTA**.

Bug reports, slowdown spots and Arthur victory screenshots are very welcome in the comments. Thanks for playing! 👻🔥
