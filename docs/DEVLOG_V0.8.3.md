# Mega Ghosts'n Goblins V0.8.3 alpha: quieter map theme, keep your weapon

Hi everyone!

A small update, made from one player's report. They tested on a PAL clone console with an EverDrive and sent two notes: one about the sound, and one idea.

## The map theme is no longer too loud

The short tune with the map at the start of each stage played about three times louder than the stage music. I measured every tune, and it was the loudest by far. It now plays at the same level as the stage music. While I was at it I evened out 15 other tunes that were louder than the rest, so the volume should no longer jump between tracks.

## Keep your weapon after a death

New in Home mode's OPTIONS: **WEAPON**.

- **KEEP AFTER DEATH** (the default): when you lose a life, you start again with the weapon you were holding, as in the sequel. No more hunting for the knife again after every death.
- **LANCE AFTER DEATH**: you get the lance back every time, as before.

When I checked how the arcade handles it, it turns out the original arcade game keeps your weapon too. So **Arcade mode now keeps it as well**, as the arcade does.

The OPTIONS screen now shows the smaller logo so that the new item fits.

## Still open

- The black square at the end of stage 2's building, and the sprite glitches before the Unicorns. I still can't reproduce them. A screenshot or a save state from that spot would help a lot.
- The freeze early in stage 1 on a Model 1 with an EverDrive Pro. If you play on real hardware, please tell me whether you still see it.

## Download & play

- **Emulators:** load the .bin in any Mega Drive / Genesis emulator with battery saves (tested in Genesis Plus GX and PicoDrive).
- **Flashcarts / real hardware:** the game saves to 8 KiB of battery SRAM on a 4 MiB cartridge, so your cart needs SRAM support at that size (most EverDrives have it).
- The version is shown at the bottom of the opening menu, so you can check which one you're on. Please mention it in your reports.

Thanks for the reports, and thanks for playing! 👻🔥
