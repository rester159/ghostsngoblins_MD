# Mega Ghosts'n Goblins V0.8.4 alpha: new Firebrand art, and he takes two hits

Hi everyone!

## Shout-out: Robot-Random and GoodLuckTrying

A big thank you to **Robot-Random** and **GoodLuckTrying** for the awesome art. 🙏 Firebrand's sprites started out as script output, and they keep getting better because of you two. In this version:

- **Firebrand climbs ladders seen from behind.** Until now the ladder showed his wall-cling pose. There are new climbing frames, and new ones for getting off at the top.
- **His wall cling is redrawn**, holding on and spitting fire.
- A few more touch-ups to his other frames.

GoodLuckTrying is now in the community credits too. That screen has a new title, written in the Ghosts'n Goblins lettering.

## Firebrand takes two hits

Firebrand's Quest and Vendetta were harsh: one hit and he was dead. Now he takes two:

- **The first hit** knocks him back, like Arthur losing his armor, and takes away his flight.
- **The second hit** kills him.
- **A wing heals him** and gives his flight back. The next wing raises his flight level as before.

In Vendetta his flaps stay even when he's hurt, because its climbs need them.

## Download & play

- **Emulators:** load the .bin in any Mega Drive / Genesis emulator with battery saves (tested in Genesis Plus GX and PicoDrive).
- **Flashcarts / real hardware:** the game saves to 8 KiB of battery SRAM on a 4 MiB cartridge, so your cart needs SRAM support at that size (most EverDrives have it).
- The version is shown at the bottom of the opening menu. Please mention it in your reports.
