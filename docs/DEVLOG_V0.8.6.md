# Mega Ghosts'n Goblins V0.8.6 alpha: your buttons, your way

Hi everyone!

## Choose your fire button

Thanks to ZeroVolt for this one. If you grew up with "first button attacks, second button jumps", the old layout (fire on B, jump on A or C) kept tripping you up.

**HOME → OPTIONS → BUTTONS** now has three layouts:

- **FIRE B**: jump with A or C. This is the default, the layout the game always had.
- **FIRE A**: jump with B or C.
- **FIRE C**: jump with A or B.

It applies to both pads in 2-player co-op and is saved with your other options. Arcade mode keeps its own layout.

## 4-WAY attack

**HOME → OPTIONS → ATTACK** can now be set to **4-WAY**:

- Hold **Up** and fire to throw the lance straight up, which is handy against the ravens and anything else coming from above.
- Hold **Down** in a jump and fire to throw it straight down onto whatever is under you.
- Down while crouching on the ground is still the normal low throw.

It's Arthur's lance only: the other weapons, Maximo in co-op and Firebrand throw as before. **NORMAL** stays the default, and with it the game plays exactly as before.

## Download & play

- **Emulators:** load the .bin in any Mega Drive / Genesis emulator with battery saves (tested in Genesis Plus GX and PicoDrive).
- **Flashcarts / real hardware:** the game saves to 8 KiB of battery SRAM on a 4 MiB cartridge, so your cart needs SRAM support at that size (most EverDrives have it).
- Both new options are under **HOME**, then **OPTIONS**.
- The version is shown at the bottom of the opening menu. Please mention it in your reports.

Thanks for playing! 👻🔥
